Why track earnings separately from your timesheet

  • Apps and employers pay on different schedules. Your log catches gaps.
  • At tax time, you have one list of what you were actually paid.
  • Compare your earnings log to each paystub before you file anything.

Reminder: this is a personal tracking tool, not tax advice. Keep your own copies and talk to a tax professional for filing help.
