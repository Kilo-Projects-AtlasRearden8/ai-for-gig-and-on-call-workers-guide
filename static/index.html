Who this is for

You drive for a rideshare app, deliver packages, work warehouse shifts, pick up nursing on-call blocks, or freelance on the side. Your schedule changes every week. This site helps you track shifts, log earnings, swap shifts, and run small automations — without assuming you work nine to five.
