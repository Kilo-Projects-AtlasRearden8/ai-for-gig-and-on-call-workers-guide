Automation that fits your life

You don't need to automate everything. Pick one annoying five-minute task and let the computer handle it. Every idea here runs on your own computer — nothing is uploaded.

1. Weekly earnings summary

One command adds up this week's hours and pay from your shift log. See tools/earnings-tracker.py in the project folder.

2. Shift reminder

The night before a shift, your screen shows tomorrow's start time and location. You set it once; it runs when you ask.

3. Message drafts

AI writes the first version of a client message, a rate-increase note, or a shift-swap request. You edit and send. Try it on the shift swap helper page.

Try it: weekly earnings summary

Paste your shifts below (one per line: date, hours, pay). This page adds the totals — nothing leaves your browser.

Keep it local-first

Your schedule and earnings are personal. Scripts in tools/ save data only to files on your computer and need no API keys or accounts.
