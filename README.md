# AI for Gig, On-Call, and Irregular-Schedule Workers

A beginner-friendly manual and static site for people whose hours aren't a
9-to-5: gig workers, on-call staff, shift workers, and anyone with an
unpredictable schedule. Built and published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.

## What's inside

- **docs/** — the beginner manual: AI basics, timesheets, earnings tracking,
  shift swaps, simple automation, local AI and privacy.
- **static/** — a small HTML site you can open in any browser or print.
- **worksheets/** — printable earnings and shift worksheets (HTML, print-ready).
- **tools/** — small local-first helper scripts (Python): timesheet logger,
  earnings tracker, and shift-swap message drafts. Runs on your computer;
  nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **tests/** — tests for the helper scripts.

## Who this is for

If your schedule changes week to week — nurses, rideshare and delivery drivers,
warehouse workers, freelancers, on-call tech support, and more — this guide is
written for you. No coding experience required. Everything is explained plain,
step by step, never condescending.
