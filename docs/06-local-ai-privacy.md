# 5. Local AI and privacy

Your work schedule, your earnings, and your clients' details are personal.
Public chatbots usually save what you type and may use it to train their models.
For anything private, keep AI on your own computer.

## Local AI options

- **Ollama** (ollama.com) — runs open-source language models on your laptop or
  desktop. Free for personal use. Nothing leaves your machine unless you choose
  to share it.
- **LM Studio** — a friendly app to download and run local models.
- **PrivateGPT / AnythingLLM** — let you chat with your own documents locally.

You don't need a powerful gaming PC. Small models run fine on most laptops made
in the last several years.

## Privacy rules of thumb

1. **Never paste real names, addresses, Social Security numbers, bank account
   numbers, or patient information into a public chatbot.** Use placeholders
   like "Client A" or "the hospital."
2. **Read before you send.** AI writes drafts; you decide what actually goes out.
3. **Check the math.** AI can miscount hours and money. Always verify earnings
   totals yourself.
4. **Keep backups.** If your shift log lives in one file on one computer, copy
   it to a USB drive or cloud storage you control once a week.

## Healthcare workers: education only

If you're a nurse, CNA, or other healthcare worker: AI can help with scheduling,
paperwork, and learning — but it cannot give medical advice, make diagnoses, or
replace your professional judgment. Follow your employer's policies and your
licensing board's rules.
