# AI for Gig, On-Call, and Irregular-Schedule Workers

**A beginner's guide to using AI when your hours aren't a 9-to-5.**
Built for unpredictable schedules: timesheets, earnings tracking, shift swaps,
and simple automation you can run on your own computer.

*Prepared by Asdale Dynamics, LLC — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and
practical tools. Written by Dylan Asdale, registered nurse turned AI author.*

---

## Who this is for

You drive for a rideshare app, deliver packages, work warehouse shifts, pick up
nursing on-call blocks, or freelance on the side. Your schedule changes every
week. Sometimes you don't know your hours until the day before. You want AI to
help, but most guides assume you sit at a desk from nine to five. This one
doesn't.

No coding experience required. Every step is written plain, like a friend would
explain it. Nothing here is medical advice — if it touches your health or your
license, talk to your employer or a qualified professional.

## A quick note on tone

This guide is written for beginners. That means no jargon without an explanation,
no assumptions about your schedule, and never talking down to you. If a step
feels too fast, slow down. You can't break anything by reading.
